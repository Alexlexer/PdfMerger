# Code of Conduct

PdfMerger contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces
welcoming, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give specific, actionable technical feedback.
- Welcome people regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's personal information.
- Accept correction, take responsibility for mistakes, and de-escalate conflict.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, and publication of private information are
not tolerated in project issues, pull requests, discussions, or other spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile
or GitHub's abuse-reporting tools. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
close discussions, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with decisions based on
context, impact, and the safety of project participants.
